# Opportunity Criteria

These are the current human preferences that the job pipeline should encode before fit/classification or CV tailoring. They are review criteria, not immutable law: if a role looks unusually strong, keep it visible for human review instead of silently dropping it.

## Location And Work Authorization

- US-remote is blocked. Treat "Remote - United States", "US only", "must be authorized to work in the United States", "US persons", or US-residency requirements as rejects unless the listing clearly says it hires globally and the US location is only a compensation/legal-entity note.
- True EU remote is fine. EU/Europe/EMEA remote roles should pass when they are genuinely remote and do not require regular local office presence.
- Occasional business meetings are fine. Annual or quarterly offsites, short team gatherings, client visits, and conference travel are acceptable.
- Switzerland is suspect. Swiss-only, Switzerland-based, Zurich/Geneva/Basel-local, or "remote within Switzerland" roles should be rejected unless the listing explicitly allows remote work from outside Switzerland or global/Europe-wide employment without Swiss residency. This is a working assumption because Swiss roles often need the employee to be in situ or locally employed; revisit if better evidence says otherwise.
- Most other non-US remote gigs are fine when the listing is genuinely remote and geography is not otherwise blocked.

## Work Mode

- Prefer remote strongly.
- Regular hybrid or office attendance is usually a reject.
- In-person/hybrid can stay in review only when the company or role is unusually interesting, especially an AI lab, frontier AI company, or serious AI research/product engineering group.
- Preferred in-person/hybrid locations, when the role clears that higher bar: Singapore, UAE, London, and other unusually interesting locales.

## UK And Contracting

- UK security clearance is a blocker. Reject roles requiring current/active SC, DV, NPPV, UK security clearance, or clearance eligibility that implies UK citizenship/residency constraints.
- Inside IR35 is a strong negative. Reject ordinary Inside IR35 contracts unless the company or role is exceptional enough to deserve human review anyway.
- London is not automatically blocked. London roles can pass when remote-friendly or exceptional enough, subject to the clearance and IR35 caveats above.

## Pipeline Interpretation

- "Remote" is work mode, not geographic scope. A listing that only says "Remote" still needs country/geography interpretation from ATS metadata and body text.
- Prefer false negatives only for hard blockers: US-only, Swiss-only with no outside-Switzerland permission, clearance-required, ordinary regular hybrid/on-site, or ordinary Inside IR35.
- For ambiguous but plausible non-US remote roles, pass into human review with the uncertainty called out.
- For exceptional AI lab/frontier AI roles, preserve borderline location cases for review instead of dropping them automatically.
