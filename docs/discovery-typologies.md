# Discovery Typologies

This is the working vocabulary for source sweeps and classification. It is deliberately broader than the final fit decision: a role can match one of these typologies and still be rejected or marked for human review because of geography, clearance, stale pages, or source quality.

The current expansion came from the internal-AI jobs board at https://internal-ai-jobs.concept.site/ and is meant to catch roles that do not advertise themselves as plain `agentic engineer`, `RAG engineer`, `FDE`, or `inference engineer`.

## Default JobServe Terms

These terms are now in the default fast-refresh JobServe lane:

- `agentic`
- `langchain`
- `forward deployed engineer`
- `inference engineer`
- `ai enablement`
- `ai automation`
- `internal ai`

Keep this list relatively small. It affects the fast path used for latest-job refreshes.

## Classification Labels

The classifier should preserve these labels separately from fit/ranking:

- `ai_enablement`: AI enablement, workforce enablement, AI tools/adoption.
- `internal_ai_tooling`: internal AI tools, internal products, internal platforms, employee productivity, developer acceleration.
- `business_automation_ai`: AI automation, workflow/process automation, Workato/Zapier/Make/n8n, agentic workflows.
- `ai_operations`: AI ops, AI operations lead/engineer/specialist.
- `workplace_ai`: workplace technology, productivity AI, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini Enterprise, collaboration tooling.
- `ai_adoption_transformation`: AI adoption, AI transformation, AI strategy, change management.

These labels are additive. They should not hide the existing labels such as `agentic_engineer`, `rag_enterprise`, `fde`, `inference_engineer`, `developer_tools`, or `backend_product_engineering`.

## Examples To Watch

Potentially relevant non-US or geography-review roles observed from the Concept board included London, UK; Remote Germany; Berlin; Argentina/South Africa remote; UK/Ireland hybrid; Dublin; Singapore; Bulgaria; Copenhagen/remote; and Tokyo/Yokohama. Treat US remote and US-only roles as low fit unless the company is exceptional.

The board is US-heavy, so use it as a typology source rather than a direct high-quality job source.
